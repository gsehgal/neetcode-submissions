### Learnings

I have been using both Gemini and NeetBot. They have definitely helped me understand the concepts a much better.
I use them in the following ways:

**1** When I get stuck in a problem I use it to understand why my approach is not working and the gap between my approach
      and the "brute force" solution. Once I understand the brute force solution then I first give attempt to optimize it
      or else will use LLM help to understand it. 

      This has definitely been much more helpful than looking at other people solutions because the LLM gives you direct inputs
      on your solution and you can brainstorm with it to understand where the gaps in your undestanding are.

**2** Even if I am able to solve the problem; I ask the LLM to analyze my solution using the following criteria
      **a** How clean is the solution
      **b** What improvements can be made
      **c** Consider yourself as a FAANG interviewer and give feedback. 
              I use this last prompt because I want to give the LLM the context of reviewing it as a person who is 
              Good at solving problems.
